# Building Career Proof Through Public Work

The market increasingly rewards demonstrated capability over self-reported skill.

## Public work creates trust

Portfolio trust comes from observable behavior:

- Problem framing quality
- Delivery consistency
- Communication clarity
- Collaboration maturity

## What to publish

1. Build logs
2. Architecture notes
3. Demo videos
4. Postmortems

## One repeatable template

| Artifact | Frequency | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build log | Weekly | Track progress |
| Demo clip | Bi-weekly | Show real functionality |
| Reflection note | Monthly | Capture lessons and trade-offs |

Consistency over hype wins.
